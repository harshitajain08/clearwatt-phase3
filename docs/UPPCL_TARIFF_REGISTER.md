The UPERC order applies to the state DISCOMs (PVVNL, MVVNL, DVVNL, PuVVNL and KESCO). “UPPCL” is an umbrella and the actual distribution licensee, supply area and consumer schedule must be identified from the bill. Do not apply a single generic UP rate table across all geographies or consumer categories.

Before activating the rules, record the effective date, rural/urban schedule, category, connected load, slab thresholds, fixed/demand charges, minimum charges, subsidies, due-date rebate, electricity duty, regulatory surcharge, monthly FPPAS and any special conditions. Keep the source PDF and page references with the tariff dataset version.
